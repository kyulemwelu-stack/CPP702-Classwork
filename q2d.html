Importance of the Alt Attribute

The alt attribute provides a text alternative for an image and helps users understand its meaning.

Example

Consequences of Missing Alt Text

1. Visually Impaired Users

Screen reader users may not receive important information conveyed through images.

2. Users with Slow Internet

When images fail to load, users may not understand what the missing images represent.

3. Search Engine Users

Search engines receive less textual information about image content, which may affect image search visibility.

4. Users with Cognitive Disabilities

Meaningful alternative text can help explain the purpose of informative images, improving understanding and accessibility.
