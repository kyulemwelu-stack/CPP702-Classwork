Absolute and Relative Hyperlinks

1. Absolute Hyperlink

An absolute hyperlink contains the complete URL, including the protocol and domain name.

It is suitable for linking to external websites such as the Central Bank of Kenya.

Visit Central Bank of Kenya

2. Relative Hyperlink

A relative hyperlink specifies the path of a page relative to the current website location.

It is suitable for linking between pages within a bank's intranet.

Staff Portal

Difference

Absolute links contain a complete web address while relative links depend on the current page location.
