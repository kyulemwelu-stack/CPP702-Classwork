Types of Lists in HTML5

1. Ordered List

An ordered list is used when items must follow a particular sequence, such as steps for student registration.

  1. Open the university website
  2. Fill in the application form
  3. Upload required documents
  4. Submit the application

2. Unordered List

An unordered list is used when the order of items is not important, such as listing university courses.

  • Information Technology
  • Business Management
  • Computer Science
  • Accounting

3. Description List

A description list is used to display terms and their definitions, such as a website glossary.
